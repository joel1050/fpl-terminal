§1–21

How FPL Terminal turns data into expected points

Every number in the app comes from one chain of steps. Live FPL data, past seasons and team news go in. Expected points (xP) for each player and fixture come out. The lineup, transfer and squad tools all build on that one number.

The whole chain on one page

In short: work out how strong each team is, how long each player will play, and how often they score per 90 minutes. Adjust for the opponent, convert to FPL points, then add those points up over the weeks you plan for.

Click any box to open its page. On a phone, scroll the diagram sideways.

Three kinds of data, kept apart

In short: the app never mixes what FPL reports with what it guesses. Each estimate traces back to the inputs that made it.

fact

Straight from the official FPL API.

  • price, ownership
  • injury status codes
  • fixtures, deadlines
  • season totals so far

evidence

Outside sources that inform a guess.

  • past-season match stats
  • RotoWire predicted XIs
  • RotoWire QUES / OUT / SUS
  • ClubElo ratings

estimate

What the model works out. Same inputs, same answer.

  • expected minutes
  • team strengths
  • xP, risk, confidence
  • nailed rating

§1Rules that hold everywhere

105 = £10.5m

Money is whole tenths of a million. Divide by 10 only to display it.

0–90

Minutes are for one fixture.

0–1

Every probability.

per 90

Every rate: xG, xA, bonus, saves, defensive contributions.

xP = mean

An average outcome, never one random draw.

1 · 3 · 5 · 10

Horizons count distinct gameweeks, not fixtures.

§4–5

Who plays, and for how long

Last season's start rate, updated match by match, then RotoWire's predicted XI and FPL's injury flag.

pn = (1 − 0.40) × p(n−1) + 0.40 × startedThisMatch
§6

Per-90 rates

How often a player does each thing per 90 minutes, pulled toward a sensible prior until enough minutes back it up.

rate = (observed × minutes + prior × 900) / (minutes + 900)
§7

Fixture adjustment

The opponent and venue scale attacking output and set the clean-sheet chance.

attackMultiplier = base × (home ? 1.102 : 0.898)
§8–9

Expected points

Each way a player can score or lose FPL points, weighted by start and cameo chances, then summed per gameweek.

total = sum of all components
